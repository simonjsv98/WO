# CLAUDE.md – ODLA

Keep this file current. When the workflow, the structure or a standing preference changes, update it in the same change.

## Workflow

- ODLA is the app on `main` in the WO repo. It is a standalone static app: `index.html` plus `scene.js` (the 3D module) and the icons.
- Every push to `main` publishes it to GitHub Pages at https://simonjsv98.github.io/WO/ (`.github/workflows/pages.yml` copies `index.html`, `scene.js` and the icon). Finish changes in the session and merge them to `main`, so the app updates.
- The old training app (WO) rests on the branch `traningsapp`. Its workflow only runs by hand; running it would replace ODLA on the Pages site.
- The page has `noindex` and opens with a password (`LOCK_HASH` in `index.html`, a PBKDF2 hash; the password itself is not in the repo, ask Simon). An unlocked device is remembered. The source is public on GitHub, so the password only keeps people out of the page.
- Talk to Simon in Swedish. The app's text is Swedish.
- Check changes in a phone-width browser (390 px) with Playwright, and on iPad Air sizes (820×1180, 1180×820) when the layout changes.
- Same look, feel and motion as RECIPES and KNIT (simonjsv98/recipes, simonjsv98/knit): Google Sans, #F3F2EF background, fixed top bar, colour tiles on the start page, a list page with a coloured title and dot, short fades between pages, swipe right to go back, sheets that slide up and down, a fixed button at the bottom, the update button with the circle wipe. The motion code is copied from KNIT; keep them in step.
- ODLA's colours: deep green #24412C with pale lime #D4EC9A (dots #8CC152) for LANDET; rust #B4502A with #FFE3D2 for SÄSONG (the colour of the rebar fence); pale sage #DCE7C5 with #24412C for DAGBOK.

## Structure

- `index.html`: everything except the 3D scene. `CROPS` (sowing, planting and harvest months for southern Sweden, family, and how the crop is drawn in 3D), `sketch()` (the garden as first sketched from Simon's photos), the views (start, LANDET, SÄSONG, DAGBOK), the map (`drawPlan`, drawn as SVG in metres) and the sheets.
- `build/scene-src.js`: the 3D model with three.js. Keep it light for phones: after a build, everything that stands still is merged into one mesh per material (`bake`), so tapping uses boxes measured before the merge (`measure`, `pickAt`), not the meshes; shadows are only redrawn when the sun, plants or garden change (`shadowMap.autoUpdate` is off, call `dirtyShadows()`); the canvas drops to 1× resolution while the camera moves; plant materials are shared per colour; far trees use a simpler crown. A slider updates plants without the growing animation. The app starts loading the module in the background after unlocking. Tapping a bed glides the camera to it (`focus`); a button shows the garden from above (`top`). `stats()` reports draw calls and triangles (about 50 and 100 000 with the sketch). Run `npm install` and `npm run build` after changing it; that writes `scene.js`. Commit the rebuilt `scene.js`.
- Coordinates are metres. x runs from the left fence to the right as seen on the map, y from the front fence (where the gate and the stone-edged border are) backwards. The map has the back up, whatever the compass says; its north arrow turns with `garden.site.orient`. The app says left and right, not west and east. In 3D the front faces the camera.
- Data is saved in localStorage under `odla-data`: `garden` (fence size, gate, beds, items), `plantings` (crop, bed, year and the stretch of the bed it takes, `from`–`to` as fractions of the bed's long side, with optional dates), `log` (the journal) and `done` (ticked tasks). It can be saved to and read from a file.
- A planting's dates (sown, planted, first harvest, cleared) decide how big it looks in 3D at the time chosen with the slider; without dates the crop's usual months are used.
- Crop rotation (`ROT_GROUPS`, `FAM_GROUP`): potatis & tomat → ärtväxter → kål & gurkväxter → rot, lök & blad. A bed's suggestion follows from what took most of it the year before.

## Sun

- `sunPos` (the SunCalc formulas) gives the sun's height and compass direction; `momentOf` turns a point in the season and a Swedish clock time (with summer time) into a timestamp; `sunDir` turns that into the garden's own axes using `garden.site.orient`, the compass bearing the back of the garden faces (72: the garden runs parallel with the barn, which is turned about 18° from north towards west, and Simon turned the sketch a quarter clockwise so its front with the gate faces the barn). Saved data from before (`siteVersion` 1, −18) is moved over.
- `garden.site` defaults to a general position (57,7 N, 12,0 E). Simon's exact position is typed in the app and kept only on the device; never put it in the repo.
- `garden.obstacles` cast the shade: buildings (`base` = the ground they stand on above the garden, `eaves`, `ridge`, gable roof along the longer side) and higher ground (`terrace`, sloping up from its `foot` edge, N/S/E/W in the garden's axes, over `ramp` metres). `shaded()` casts a ray from a point towards the sun; `sunHours()` samples each bed every 40 cm, every ten minutes from sunrise to sunset, and splits the hours before and after the sun passes south. Results are cached until the garden changes.
- The default obstacles are measured on Simon's aerial photo of the garden just after it was laid out, with the barn's length (19 m) as the scale: the barn about 19 × 10,5 m, 10 m to the ridge, on ground 3 m above the garden; its long side parallel with the garden's, its wall about 3,5 m in front of the front fence; the garden starts about 16 m along the barn and reaches about 7 m past its far gable, so the barn stands behind the garden's left end (north-west of it). The bank between them is about 2,2 m wide, its foot 1,2 m in front of the fence. On that photo the brown, newly dug area is about 10 × 8,5 m; the fence may be smaller, so the garden's own size waits for Simon's measurements. `siteVersion` 3 moves saved data over. These are guesses until Simon measures. The barn is called "Byggnaden i väster" in the app, not by Simon's name for it, which is part of the password.
- Tapping a bed in 3D, on the map or in the list marks it (a yellow ring in 3D, a yellow outline on the map with the others faded) and shows a small card over the stage (`pick`, `cardBody`, `refreshCard`). The garden stays visible and usable; the card's buttons open the full sheet.
- `omgivning.json`: trees and ground around the garden from Lantmäteriet's open height data (CC BY 4.0, credit them in the app): Laserdata Nedladdning skog (point cloud, March 2022) and Markhöjdmodell Nedladdning (1 m). 140 × 140 m in 1 m cells centred on the garden's centre, in the world's directions (row 0 north), heights in 0,2 m steps as base64 bytes: `ground` (zero within 30 m, where the site has been dug since) and `trees` (from 2,5 m; low things within 32 m and everything within 10 m of the centre left out, since the barn and the garden were built after the scan). No coordinates are stored. The app loads it at start (`SUR`); `shadedBySurround()` marches a ray through it, and the 3D scene draws a crown every 2 m (`scene.surround`). It is published with the app (the Pages workflow copies it).
- Simon has a Geotorget account with the free Markhöjdmodell and Laserdata skog API products; the session's environment holds the login for dl1.lantmateriet.se (the proxy adds it). The STAC catalogue is https://api.lantmateriet.se/stac-hojd/v1 (collections `dtm-cog`, `dsm-skoglig-copc`). Ythöjdmodell från flygbilder costs 371 000 kr a year: never order it. The laser scan predates the barn, so the barn and the bank stay modelled by hand from the aerial photo.
- `SUN_NEED` is the hours of direct sun each crop wants; a bed warns when it gets less in July.
- In 3D the sun follows the date and the clock slider; the map's "Sol per bädd" colours the beds by hours of sun for the chosen month.
